Three roasts, one shared obsession with flavor.

Sunrise Light Roast

Bright and floral, with notes of stone fruit and honey. Best brewed as a pour-over.

Highland Medium Roast

Balanced and nutty, with a soft caramel finish. Our most popular everyday blend.

Midnight Dark Roast

Bold and smoky, with a rich chocolate depth. Holds up well to milk.

We started with one roaster and a lot of stubbornness.

Terra Coffee Co. opened in 2019 with a single drum roaster and a belief that coffee tastes better when you know exactly where it came from. Today we work with six farming cooperatives across three countries, and we still roast everything ourselves, in small batches, every week.
